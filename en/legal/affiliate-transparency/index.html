Affiliate transparency

How affiliate links may fund BUSCO! without determining search relevance.

Version applicable to buscooficial.com · 8 October 2026

1. Affiliate links

Some BUSCO! links participate in affiliate programmes. If you click and later purchase, BUSCO! may receive a commission from the provider or affiliate network.

2. What users need to know

BUSCO! identifies affiliate relationships where appropriate. It is not necessary to publish the exact private commission received for every sale unless a specific legal or programme requirement says otherwise.

3. Search independence

Affiliate status does not by itself determine search order or fit. Non-affiliate tools may be included when relevant, and affiliate tools may be absent when they do not match the query.

4. Sponsored content

If BUSCO! later publishes a placement or content item specifically paid for by a brand, it will be clearly identified as sponsored or advertising.
