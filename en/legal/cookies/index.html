Cookie & storage policy

How BUSCO! uses necessary browser storage and optional measurement.

Version applicable to buscooficial.com · 8 October 2026

1. Browser storage

BUSCO! mainly uses localStorage and sessionStorage. These are not always traditional cookies, but this policy groups them because they perform similar device-storage functions.

2. Necessary and functional storage

busco_consent remembers your measurement choice; language and intro preferences remember how you use the interface. These do not support behavioural advertising.

3. Optional measurement

Only after you select “Accept measurement” can BUSCO! create random analytics identifiers and record aggregate interaction events. Search text is not stored; only query length is recorded. Selecting “Necessary only” removes BUSCO!’s optional analytics identifiers.

4. Changing your choice

Use the persistent “Cookies” button at any time. The choice is renewed at most every 24 months, or earlier if purposes/providers materially change.

5. Third parties

External tools, WhatsApp and affiliate networks may use their own storage after you leave BUSCO!. Their policies then apply.
