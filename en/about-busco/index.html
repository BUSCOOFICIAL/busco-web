ABOUT BUSCO!

BUSCO! starts with what you want to achieve

You do not need to know a software name before you begin. BUSCO! organises AI, software and digital services around real needs and helps you compare options with more context.

The problem we are trying to solve

The digital-tool market is full of products promising to do everything. If you simply need to solve one problem, navigating hundreds of names, plans and features becomes another job. BUSCO! works in the opposite direction: explain what you want to achieve first, then compare relevant options.

What you can do in BUSCO!

Search in your own words, compare multiple tools, review individual profiles, listen to summaries and ask for help when you need to choose or implement a tool. If nothing fits, BUSCO! can also help create a solution around a real business workflow.

People behind the comparison

BUSCO! is not intended to be an anonymous automated list. The catalogue is reviewed, profiles carry review dates and the methodology explains how strengths, limitations, pricing and affiliate relationships are handled. Partner status should not change search ranking by fit.

How BUSCO! is funded

Some links may generate a commission if someone buys through BUSCO!, without changing the user's price. That commercial relationship is kept separate from the search logic and editorial comparison. BUSCO! also offers implementation support and custom development.

Our editorial principle

We would rather mark something as unconfirmed than turn an assumption into a benefit. Providers change pricing, plans and features, so profiles include review dates and current terms should be checked before buying.
