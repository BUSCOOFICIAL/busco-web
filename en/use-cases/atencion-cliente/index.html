Customer support and helpdesk tools

Compare live chat, helpdesk, ticketing, shared inbox, automation and AI tools for handling customer support with more context.

When customer questions arrive through different channels, continuity becomes the problem. Look for history, routing and automation without losing control.

What to compare: task fit; ease of use; automation; integrations; limits; scaling cost.

How to decide if it fits

Define a real use case, test two or three options and check what data must move, who will use them and what they cost when volume grows. Avoid deciding from one eye-catching feature.
