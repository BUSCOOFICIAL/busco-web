Tools for WhatsApp automation and management

Compare tools for centralising WhatsApp conversations, automating messages and organising support or sales.

WhatsApp becomes a bottleneck when conversations depend on one person. Look for a workflow that keeps context, permissions and traceability without turning every chat into a robotic sequence.

What to compare: task fit; ease of use; automation; integrations; limits; scaling cost.

How to decide if it fits

Define a real use case, test two or three options and check what data must move, who will use them and what they cost when volume grows. Avoid deciding from one eye-catching feature.
