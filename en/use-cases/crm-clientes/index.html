CRM tools for customers and follow-up

Compare CRM tools for organising customers, contacts, leads, pipelines and sales follow-up without losing opportunities.

If customer information is spread across WhatsApp, email, notes and spreadsheets, a CRM can create one view of follow-up. The key is choosing something your team will actually keep updated.

What to compare: task fit; ease of use; automation; integrations; limits; scaling cost.

How to decide if it fits

Define a real use case, test two or three options and check what data must move, who will use them and what they cost when volume grows. Avoid deciding from one eye-catching feature.
