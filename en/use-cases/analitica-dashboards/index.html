Analytics, dashboard and KPI tools

Compare tools for bringing data together, building dashboards, monitoring KPI and understanding business performance.

Start with the questions you need to answer. Then check whether the tool connects your sources, refreshes often enough and presents indicators people will actually use.

What to compare: task fit; ease of use; automation; integrations; limits; scaling cost.

How to decide if it fits

Define a real use case, test two or three options and check what data must move, who will use them and what they cost when volume grows. Avoid deciding from one eye-catching feature.
