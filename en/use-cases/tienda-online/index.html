Tools for building an online store

Compare ecommerce platforms for selling products or services, managing payments, catalogues and growth.

An online store is more than a website with a cart. Compare catalogue management, payments, shipping, fees, integrations and the control you need over design and data.

What to compare: task fit; ease of use; automation; integrations; limits; scaling cost.

How to decide if it fits

Define a real use case, test two or three options and check what data must move, who will use them and what they cost when volume grows. Avoid deciding from one eye-catching feature.
