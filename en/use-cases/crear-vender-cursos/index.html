Platforms for creating and selling online courses

Compare platforms for building courses, managing learners, taking payments and running an online academy.

Before choosing, separate content creation, selling, learner experience and community. Some platforms cover everything; others specialise in one part.

What to compare: task fit; ease of use; automation; integrations; limits; scaling cost.

How to decide if it fits

Define a real use case, test two or three options and check what data must move, who will use them and what they cost when volume grows. Avoid deciding from one eye-catching feature.
