Manychat: what it does, pricing and alternatives

A conversational marketing automation platform for capturing leads, talking to customers and selling through Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok, Facebook Messenger, Telegram, SMS and email. It automates DMs, comment replies, lead capture, sequences, broadcasts and Inbox workflows.

What it is useful for

automatically reply to comments and DMs; turn social engagement into leads; deliver links and resources by DM; capture emails and phone numbers; automate WhatsApp; build sequences and broadcasts; centralise conversations; segment contacts; follow up; use AI to reply and guide conversations

Who it suits

creators, influencers, ecommerce brands, social media marketers, agencies, coaches, trainers and businesses that acquire customers through social media and messaging

Main features

Core capabilities: automatically reply to comments and DMs; turn social engagement into leads; deliver links and resources by DM; capture emails and phone numbers; automate WhatsApp; build sequences and broadcasts; centralise conversations; segment contacts; follow up; use AI to reply and guide conversations.

Artificial intelligence

AI features are included; scope and limits depend on the product and plan described by the provider.

Pricing, free plan and trial

New pricing model introduced on 2 March 2026. Current prices shown: Free $0; Essential from $14/month; Pro from $29/month; Business from $69/month; Advanced from $139/month. Monthly and annual billing are available, with annual discounts of up to 30%. Cost also scales with active contacts. Free plan: A free plan is available. Limits and included features depend on the provider’s current plan conditions. Trial: A trial or test period is available; duration and conditions depend on the provider’s current offer.

Strengths

strong specialisation in Instagram and social messaging; multichannel ecosystem; free plan; comment-to-DM automations; WhatsApp; built-in AI; Inbox; broad integration ecosystem; well-known among creators and marketers

Things to consider

new pricing is based on Active Contacts and can rise with volume; WhatsApp template messages can add extra costs; some features and channels depend on the plan; older accounts may temporarily remain on the previous pricing model

Integrations and environment

Integrations: Google Sheets, Zapier, ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Hotmart, Klaviando, ActiveCampaign, HubSpot CRM, Kit, Mailchimp, Flodesk and Meta CAPI, between other. Platform: Web service and mobile app for Inbox/management. Officially supported channels include Instagram, TikTok, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS and email, with availability depending on the plan. Languages: Spanish is supported; the exact interface and content coverage depends on the product.

What makes it different

deep automation around social interactions, especially Instagram, combined with multiple messaging channels, AI and a shared Inbox

How to decide if it fits

Manychat may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
