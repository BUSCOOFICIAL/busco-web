Marquorum: what it does, pricing and alternatives

An affiliate network and creator programme built around a portfolio of subscription-based AI apps. Marquorum develops or operates the offers, manages links, attribution, commissions and payouts, and lets eligible partners vote on future product opportunities.

Pricing, free plan and trial

There is no published affiliate subscription as the main product. Portfolio products have their own prices. Revenue Scout, the current visible offer, shows Free €0 in continental Europe; Starter €99/month; Growth €349/month; Scale €999/month. Free plan: A free plan is available. Limits and included features depend on the provider’s current plan conditions. Trial: A trial or test period is available; duration and conditions depend on the provider’s current offer.

Strengths

30% lifetime recurring commission; second-tier commission; several offers under one account; built-in attribution and dashboard; monthly payouts; Spain supported; eligible partners can help influence future products

Things to consider

the brand and portfolio are less established than major SaaS networks; attractiveness depends on the quality and demand of each app; catalogue and terms may evolve; some offers have relatively high price points

What makes it different

How to decide if it fits

Marquorum may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
