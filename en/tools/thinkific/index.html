Thinkific: what it does, pricing and alternatives

A platform for creating, selling and managing online courses, communities, memberships, digital products, coaching and webinars. It includes content creation, websites and landing pages, payments, marketing, analytics and tools for scaling an education business.

Who it suits

creators, trainers, coaches, experts, academies, associations, companies and teams that sell education or use training for customers, partners or employees

Main features

Core capabilities: create courses; sell training; host videos and lessons; communities and memberships; certificates; quizzes and assessments; coaching and webinars; landing pages; payments; subscriptions; bundles; affiliates; analytics; customer or employee training.

Artificial intelligence

AI features are included; scope and limits depend on the product and plan described by the provider.

Pricing, free plan and trial

Basic $49/month; Start $99/month; Grow $199/month; Thinkific Plus uses custom pricing. Annual billing offers a discount compared with monthly billing. Prices and promotions may vary, so BUSCO should date the pricing shown. Free plan: No permanent free plan is confirmed in the current review. Trial: A trial or test period is available; duration and conditions depend on the provider’s current offer.

Strengths

mature platform; courses plus communities and memberships; sales tools; App Store; payments; certificates and assessments; mobile learning; B2B and enterprise options; branding and scalability

Things to consider

cost rises with features and scale; some advanced capabilities, API access, branded mobile or enterprise features require higher plans or add-ons; an old Free plan should not be treated as current unless it appears in official pricing; processing fees depend on method and region

Integrations and environment

Integrations: Thinkific App Store. Integrations/documentation official incluanden Zoom, Zapier, Mailchimp, ConvertKit/Kit, ActiveCampaign, Shopifand, Stripe/PayPal according to withtexto, analandtics and multiple apps of marketing, automation and aprendizaje. Platform: Web/cloud application. Thinkific Mobile supports the learner experience on iOS/Android on compatible offers. Branded Mobile may be available as an add-on or higher-tier solution. Languages: Spanish is supported; the exact interface and content coverage depends on the product.

What makes it different

combines a business-focused LMS with direct sales, communities, memberships, an App Store and a clear path from solo creator to enterprise learning

How to decide if it fits

Thinkific may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
