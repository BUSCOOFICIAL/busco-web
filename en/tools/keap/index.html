Keap: what it does, pricing and alternatives

A small-business platform combining CRM, contact follow-up, marketing and payments, with automation built around the journey from lead capture to sale.

What it is useful for

Follow up leads; automate communications; book appointments; invoice for services.

Who it suits

Small service businesses, consultants, coaches and sales teams.

Main features

Core capabilities: Follow up leads; automate communications; book appointments; invoice for services.

Artificial intelligence

AI is not confirmed as a core feature in the current review.

Pricing, free plan and trial

299 USD/month or 2.988 USD/year, equivalent a 249 USD/month. the offer includes condiciones of compromiso and cancelación; cuotas extra by users/contacts: NOT CONFIRMED. Free plan: No permanent free plan is confirmed in the current review. Trial: A trial or test period is available; duration and conditions depend on the provider’s current offer.

Strengths

Brings acquisition and sales together; automates follow-up; combines scheduling and payments.

Things to consider

Higher starting cost than basic CRMs; trial restrictions; cancelling an annual commitment early may incur a USD 299 fee and requires notice. Business Line is limited to the US and Canada and supports numbers from those countries.

Integrations and environment

Integrations: Marketplace and withexiones with apps enterprise; API for desarrolladores. Listado withcreto included at no cost: NOT CONFIRMED. Platform: Web and apps iOS and Android. Languages: Spanish support is not fully confirmed in the current review.

What makes it different

Designed for businesses that want to automate the full customer journey in one platform.

How to decide if it fits

Keap may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
