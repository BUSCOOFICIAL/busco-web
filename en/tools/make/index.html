Make: what it does, pricing and alternatives

A visual no-code/low-code automation platform for connecting apps, data and processes. It lets you build automated scenarios and add AI and agents directly into workflows.

What it is useful for

automate repetitive tasks; sync data; connect tools; automate marketing, sales, operations, support, finance and HR; build AI workflows; integrate APIs; orchestrate agents

Who it suits

self-employed professionals and advanced creators, SMEs, marketing, sales and operations teams, agencies, automation consultants, technical teams and larger organisations

Main features

Core capabilities: automate repetitive tasks; sync data; connect tools; automate marketing, sales, operations, support, finance and HR; build AI workflows; integrate APIs; orchestrate agents.

Artificial intelligence

AI features are included; scope and limits depend on the product and plan described by the provider.

Pricing, free plan and trial

Free $0; Core $12/month; Pro $21/month; Teams $38/month; Enterprise custom pricing. The website indicates savings of 15% or more with annual billing. Price scales with credits. Free plan: A free plan is available. Limits and included features depend on the provider’s current plan conditions. Trial: No standard time-limited trial is confirmed; check whether a free plan or demo is available.

Strengths

huge integration ecosystem; visual builder; complex automation without heavy coding; free plan; API and custom apps; built-in AI and agents; scales from individual users to enterprise teams

Things to consider

the credit system can make costs harder to estimate; complex workflows have a learning curve; Free is limited to 1,000 credits and 15-minute scheduling; advanced features depend on the plan

Integrations and environment

Integrations: 000+ apps verified. examples: OpenAI, HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, Canva, Google, Microsoft, Shopifand, WooCommerce, Stripe, Telegram, Pipedrive, Zendesk, etc. Platform: Confirmed web/cloud service; API available on paid plans. Native desktop/mobile apps: NOT CONFIRMED in this review. Languages: Spanish support is not fully confirmed in the current review.

What makes it different

powerful visual automation with 3,000+ apps, low-code flexibility, AI and agents while keeping the workflow logic and execution highly visible

How to decide if it fits

Make may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
