Coursebox: what it does, pricing and alternatives

An AI course builder and LMS. It generates courses from prompts or documents and lets you deliver, sell and manage training from your own platform.

What it is useful for

create courses quickly with AI; turn PDF, PPT or DOC files into training; sell courses; train customers or employees; AI tutoring; assessments; certificates; SCORM; branded portal; mobile app; analytics

Who it suits

course creators, small businesses, academies, learning managers, consultants, organisations and companies that need an LMS

Main features

Core capabilities: create courses quickly with AI; turn PDF, PPT or DOC files into training; sell courses; train customers or employees; AI tutoring; assessments; certificates; SCORM; branded portal; mobile app; analytics.

Artificial intelligence

AI features are included; scope and limits depend on the product and plan described by the provider.

Pricing, free plan and trial

Current official pricing in USD with annual billing: Free $0; Creator $25/month ($300/year); Plus $83/month ($1,000/year); Pro $250/month ($3,000/year); Business $583/month ($7,000/year); Enterprise custom pricing. The monthly billing selector may change the displayed prices. Free plan: A free plan is available. Limits and included features depend on the provider’s current plan conditions. Trial: A trial or test period is available; duration and conditions depend on the provider’s current offer.

Strengths

deep AI integration; fast course creation; free plan; unlimited learners; document conversion; AI tutor and grading; course sales; SCORM; white-label options; enterprise features

Things to consider

AI credits are limited by plan; Free includes Coursebox branding; full branding and custom domains increase the cost significantly; branded mobile app costs USD 3,000/year; Enterprise requires a minimum initial two-year contract

Integrations and environment

Integrations: API on Business; dynamic SCORM and SCORM export. Additional integrations and capabilities depend on the plan. Check the current official catalogue before publishing. Platform: Web/cloud. A fully branded mobile app is available as an add-on on Pro and higher plans for USD 3,000/year according to current pricing. Languages: Spanish is supported; the exact interface and content coverage depends on the product.

What makes it different

combines multimodal AI course generation with an LMS, course sales, AI tutoring, AI grading and white-label deployment

How to decide if it fits

Coursebox may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
