Hostinger: what it does, pricing and alternatives

What it is useful for

host websites; build sites with AI or WordPress; register domains; get SSL, email, backups and CDN; run ecommerce; scale projects

Who it suits

individuals, self-employed professionals, SMEs, creators, bloggers, ecommerce businesses, developers and agencies

Main features

Core capabilities: host websites; build sites with AI or WordPress; register domains; get SSL, email, backups and CDN; run ecommerce; scale projects.

Artificial intelligence

AI features are included; scope and limits depend on the product and plan described by the provider.

Pricing, free plan and trial

Single €1.49/month for 48 months, renewing at €6.99/month; Premium €2.59/month, renewing at €9.99/month; Unlimited €3.79/month, renewing at €16.99/month; Cloud Startup €7.99/month, renewing at €23.99/month. These are promotional prices tied to long commitments and may change. Free plan: No permanent free plan is confirmed in the current review. Trial: No standard time-limited trial is confirmed; check whether a free plan or demo is available.

Strengths

very competitive introductory pricing; domain, email and SSL depending on plan; built-in AI; WordPress; backups and CDN; 24/7 support; well-known brand

Things to consider

promotional pricing is tied to longer terms; renewals are substantially more expensive; features vary significantly by plan; initial and renewal prices should always be shown together

Integrations and environment

Integrations: WordPress, ecommerce and the broader web ecosystem. Exact integrations depend on the selected product; hosting supports compatible external technologies and tools. Platform: web/cloud; management from panel own and services partners. Languages: Spanish is supported; the exact interface and content coverage depends on the product.

What makes it different

combines aggressive pricing, hosting, AI, WordPress and a website builder in an ecosystem aimed strongly at beginners and SMEs

How to decide if it fits

Hostinger may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
