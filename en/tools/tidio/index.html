Tidio: what it does, pricing and alternatives

A customer service and automation platform combining live chat, helpdesk and ticketing, automated flows and Lyro, its AI customer service agent.

What it is useful for

reply to chats; centralise conversations; automate FAQs; resolve tickets with AI; capture leads; recommend products; reduce support workload; provide 24/7 support

Who it suits

ecommerce businesses, SMEs, support and customer service teams, SaaS companies and digital businesses, especially Shopify stores and teams that want automated support without a heavyweight enterprise stack

Main features

Core capabilities: reply to chats; centralise conversations; automate FAQs; resolve tickets with AI; capture leads; recommend products; reduce support workload; provide 24/7 support.

Artificial intelligence

AI features are included; scope and limits depend on the product and plan described by the provider.

Pricing, free plan and trial

Tidio currently uses a modular pricing structure with Customer Service plans and Lyro AI Agent. Prices vary by conversation volume, usage and billing cycle. The reviewed official page shows Starter, Growth, Plus and Premium options, while Lyro is priced according to the number of AI conversations. BUSCO should use the live pricing selector when publishing prices. Free plan: A free plan is available. Limits and included features depend on the provider’s current plan conditions. Trial: A trial or test period is available; duration and conditions depend on the provider’s current offer.

Strengths

combines chat, helpdesk, automation and an AI agent; accessible for SMEs; strong ecommerce integrations; free plan; scales from manual support to automation

Things to consider

cost can rise with volume; Lyro is billed by AI conversations; some advanced features require higher plans; plan structure has changed over time, so older third-party pricing reviews may be outdated

Integrations and environment

Integrations: Shopify, WordPress, WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace and other integrations/apps. Exact availability depends on the product and plan. Platform: Web app plus desktop/mobile apps for managing conversations, with a website widget for visitors. Languages: Spanish is supported; the exact interface and content coverage depends on the product.

What makes it different

an accessible option for ecommerce and SMEs that want to combine live chat with a ready-to-use AI support agent without adopting a heavy enterprise suite

How to decide if it fits

Tidio may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
