Pricing, free plan and trial

Free plan: No permanent free plan is confirmed in the current review. Trial: A trial or test period is available; duration and conditions depend on the provider’s current offer.

Strengths

Brings the training business together; combines product and marketing; mobile learner experience.

Things to consider

Significant subscription cost; plan limits apply; white-label or custom-app options and payment costs may be separate.

Integrations and environment

Integrations: API and webhooks; Zapier; withexiones of email, payments and analítica. Platform: admin web; Kajabi Mobile App and option Branded Mobile App. No equiparar app of students with editor full. Languages: Spanish support is not fully confirmed in the current review.

What makes it different

Brings knowledge products and the tools used to sell them together in one platform.

How to decide if it fits

Kajabi may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
