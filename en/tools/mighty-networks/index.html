Pricing, free plan and trial

The reviewed pricing view shows Launch $79/month, Scale $179/month and Growth $354/month; it also shows Launch at $950/year. Exact billing cadence and rounding for all cards: NOT CONFIRMED. Mighty Pro is custom-priced. Free plan: No permanent free plan is confirmed in the current review. Trial: A trial or test period is available; duration and conditions depend on the provider’s current offer.

Strengths

Community and learning in one place; recurring payments; mobile experience. [Editorial assessment]

Things to consider

Host subscription; branded apps are a separate offer; costs and limits vary by plan.

Integrations and environment

Integrations: Zoom, Kit, Zapier, Delphi and píxeles of follow-up. Platform: Web and apps mobilees native; Mighty Pro offers apps with brand. compatibility and versiones exactas NOT CONFIRMED. Languages: Spanish support is not fully confirmed in the current review.

What makes it different

The member community and relationships are the core of the experience, not just the learning content.

How to decide if it fits

Mighty Networks may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
