Pricing, free plan and trial

Semrush has moved to a modular architecture. SEO + AI Search: SEO $139/month ($117.33/month annually); Starter $199 ($165.17 annually); Pro+ $299 ($248.17 annually); Advanced $549 ($455.67 annually). The classic SEO Toolkit remains documented at Pro $139.95/month, Guru $249.95 and Business $499.95. Standalone toolkits include AI Visibility from $99/month per domain, Content from $60/month annually, Social from $20/month annually, Advertising from $99/month annually, and Traffic & Market from $289/month annually. Prices and bundles may change. Free plan: A free plan is available. Limits and included features depend on the provider’s current plan conditions. Trial: A trial or test period is available; duration and conditions depend on the provider’s current offer.

Strengths

highly recognised SEO brand; deep datasets; covers many search intents; SEO plus AI visibility; modular suite; strong competitive research; high commercial value for affiliate conversions

Things to consider

expensive compared with basic tools; complex product and pricing structure; some toolkits are sold separately; project, keyword, prompt, user and API limits depend on the plan

Integrations and environment

Integrations: Google Analandtics, Google Search Console, Looker Studio in plans compatible and multiple integrations external/reporting. API in tiers avanzados. Platform: Primarily web/cloud. Companion apps or tools may have their own availability. Languages: Spanish is supported; the exact interface and content coverage depends on the product.

What makes it different

combines a mature SEO suite with a strong focus on visibility in AI engines and several marketing toolkits in one ecosystem

How to decide if it fits

Semrush may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
