Zoho: what it does, pricing and alternatives

A broad ecosystem of business applications for sales, marketing, finance and operations. Zoho CRM is one application within it, while Zoho One bundles many products together.

What it is useful for

Centralise customers; connect departments; automate tasks; bring business applications together.

Who it suits

SMEs and companies with several management areas, plus consultants and implementation partners.

Main features

Core capabilities: Centralise customers; connect departments; automate tasks; bring business applications together.

Artificial intelligence

AI features are included; scope and limits depend on the product and plan described by the provider.

Pricing, free plan and trial

Zoho does not have a single price. The CRM calculator shows Standard $14, Professional $23, Enterprise $40 and Ultimate $52 per user/month; the billing period for that view was NOT CONFIRMED. The pricing page also returned regional INR prices that should not be applied directly to Spain. Pricing for Zoho One and other apps: NOT CONFIRMED. Free plan: A free plan is available. Limits and included features depend on the provider’s current plan conditions. Trial: No standard time-limited trial is confirmed; check whether a free plan or demo is available.

Strengths

Very broad business-app catalogue; free CRM for small teams; strong integration possibilities within the suite.

Things to consider

You need to choose the right application and edition; plans and limits are not uniform; not every later purchase is commissionable.

Integrations and environment

Integrations: other apps Zoho; Google Ads; ecosystem Marketplace. Condiciones and withectores withcretos according to app. Platform: CRM web and mobile; other apps have its propias platforms. Languages: Spanish is supported; the exact interface and content coverage depends on the product.

What makes it different

A way to bring many business functions under one provider instead of treating CRM as an isolated tool.

How to decide if it fits

Zoho may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
