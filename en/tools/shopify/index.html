Shopify: what it does, pricing and alternatives

A commerce platform for building and running online stores, selling in person and across multiple channels, and managing products, inventory, payments, orders, marketing, analytics and operations.

What it is useful for

Build an online store; sell physical or digital products; take payments; manage inventory and orders; sell through social channels, marketplaces and physical stores; expand internationally and scale.

Who it suits

Entrepreneurs, SMEs, ecommerce businesses, DTC brands, retailers, creators, B2B companies and larger organisations using Shopify Plus.

Main features

Core capabilities: Build an online store; sell physical or digital products; take payments; manage inventory and orders; sell through social channels, marketplaces and physical stores; expand internationally and scale.

Artificial intelligence

AI features are included; scope and limits depend on the product and plan described by the provider.

Pricing, free plan and trial

Basic 39 USD/month monthly or 29 USD/month with annual; Grow 105/79 USD; Advanced 399/299 USD; Plus from 2.300 USD/month in the table global localised. Shopify also publica referencias in euros for Spain that may vary according to billing/promotion, by lo that BUSCO should show the currency and billing option exactas to the publish. Free plan: No permanent free plan is confirmed in the current review. Trial: A trial or test period is available; duration and conditions depend on the provider’s current offer.

Strengths

A leading ecommerce brand with a huge ecosystem, strong scalability, omnichannel selling, POS, international features, built-in AI, APIs and high buyer awareness.

Things to consider

Monthly subscription plus possible app costs; payment and transaction fees depend on method and plan; advanced customisation may require development; Shopify Plus is expensive.

Integrations and environment

Integrations: ecosystem of miles of apps, networks social, marketplaces, payments, logística, marketing and APIs of Shopifand. Platform: web/cloud, online store, POS and apps mobilees/services partners. Languages: Spanish is supported; the exact interface and content coverage depends on the product.

What makes it different

A complete commerce ecosystem that can support anything from a small online store to retail, B2B and enterprise, backed by strong checkout, payments, apps, POS and AI infrastructure.

How to decide if it fits

Shopify may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
