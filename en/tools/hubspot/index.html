HubSpot: what it does, pricing and alternatives

A customer platform built around Smart CRM, bringing together marketing, sales, customer service, content, data and revenue or commerce tools, with Breeze AI and AI agents layered on top.

What it is useful for

CRM; lead generation; email marketing; automation; sales and pipeline management; prospecting; customer service and help desk; content and web; reporting; data; AI across the customer lifecycle

Who it suits

self-employed professionals and SMEs through Free and Starter tools; marketing, sales and service teams; scale-ups and larger companies using Professional or Enterprise

Main features

Core capabilities: CRM; lead generation; email marketing; automation; sales and pipeline management; prospecting; customer service and help desk; content and web; reporting; data; AI across the customer lifecycle.

Artificial intelligence

AI features are included; scope and limits depend on the product and plan described by the provider.

Pricing, free plan and trial

Modular pricing by Hub and tier. In the current offer, Customer Platform Free is $0 for up to 2 users; Starter is shown from $7/user/month under the current offer (reference price without promotion: $20). Professional Customer Platform starts from $1,300/month annually ($1,450 with monthly billing shown); Enterprise starts from $4,700/month. Individual Hubs have their own pricing. In Spain/EUR, the configurator shows, for example, Marketing Hub Starter at €20/month, Professional at €880/month and Enterprise at €3,300/month. Check the official configurator before publishing a fixed price. Free plan: A free plan is available. Limits and included features depend on the provider’s current plan conditions. Trial: A trial or test period is available; duration and conditions depend on the provider’s current offer.

Strengths

free CRM; very broad ecosystem; marketing, sales, service, content and data in one platform; strong integration marketplace; extensive learning resources; built-in AI; scales from microbusiness to enterprise

Things to consider

complex pricing structure; Professional and Enterprise can become expensive, especially with contacts and seats; mandatory paid onboarding applies to some plans; some AI features consume HubSpot Credits

Integrations and environment

Integrations: broad App Marketplace and withectividad with Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack and many tools comerciales, of marketing and service. APIs available. Platform: Web/cloud plus the HubSpot mobile app for iOS/Android. Breeze Assistant is also available on mobile. Languages: Spanish is supported; the exact interface and content coverage depends on the product.

What makes it different

one shared Smart CRM for multiple teams, with a broad suite and an AI layer connected to customer data

How to decide if it fits

HubSpot may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
