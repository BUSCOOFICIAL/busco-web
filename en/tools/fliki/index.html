Fliki: what it does, pricing and alternatives

An AI video and audio creation platform that turns ideas, scripts, text, URLs, presentations and images into videos. It combines synthetic voices, avatars, image and video generation, translation, dubbing, subtitles and editing in one workspace.

What it is useful for

create videos from text or ideas; generate voiceovers; repurpose blogs, PPTs and PDFs; create social and marketing videos; translate and dub video; generate avatars, images and thumbnails; produce e-learning and corporate content

Who it suits

content creators, marketing and social media teams, small businesses, corporate teams, trainers, e-learning teams, ecommerce businesses and professionals who need to produce or localise video without a traditional editing workflow

Pricing, free plan and trial

Free $0. Standard: $28/month on monthly billing according to the official Spanish pricing page reviewed. Premium: $88/month. Annual billing applies a 25% discount. Affiliate materials use Standard at $252/year as an example; check current pricing before publication. Free plan: A free plan is available. Limits and included features depend on the provider’s current plan conditions. Trial: No standard time-limited trial is confirmed; check whether a free plan or demo is available.

Strengths

all-in-one AI video workflow; 2,000+ voices; broad language coverage; text, idea, PPT and image to video; translation and dubbing; free plan; many use cases; access to several AI models in one product

Things to consider

the free plan offers only a few minutes and includes a watermark; advanced capabilities require paid plans; costs rise with heavy production; some features and models may use credits or have limits

Integrations and environment

Integrations: A formal third-party integrations catalogue was NOT CONFIRMED in the current research. Fliki does support multiple input workflows such as text, URLs, PPT/PDF, images and audio/video. Platform: Confirmed web application. Native Windows/macOS/iOS/Android apps: NOT CONFIRMED in this review. Languages: Spanish is supported; the exact interface and content coverage depends on the product.

What makes it different

combines video generation, 2,000+ voices, translation and dubbing, avatars, images and editing in one workspace with especially broad language coverage

How to decide if it fits

Fliki may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
