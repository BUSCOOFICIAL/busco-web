Strengths

Combines visual building with code; supports self-hosting; gives deep control over workflow logic.

Things to consider

Self-hosting requires technical operations; billing is execution-based; licensing and commercial uses need to be reviewed for each case.

Integrations and environment

Integrations: more of 500 integrations; HTTP, GraphQL, webhooks, APIs and código for ampliar withexiones. Platform: Editor web; Cloud or instalación own. No it confirma a app mobile native. Languages: Spanish support is not fully confirmed in the current review.

What makes it different

Especially strong for automations that need custom logic or access to your own systems.

How to decide if it fits

n8n may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
