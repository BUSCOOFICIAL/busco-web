Sight AI: what it does, pricing and alternatives

An AI-powered SEO and GEO platform for researching opportunities, creating and refreshing content, measuring brand visibility in AI search, running outreach, publishing to CMS platforms and indexing content.

What it is useful for

rank in Google and AI search; identify content gaps; monitor mentions and citations; generate articles; manage internal linking; refresh content; run outreach; publish and index automatically

Who it suits

SEO and marketing teams, SaaS companies, ecommerce businesses, agencies, publishers and blogs, local businesses and affiliates

Main features

Core capabilities: rank in Google and AI search; identify content gaps; monitor mentions and citations; generate articles; manage internal linking; refresh content; run outreach; publish and index automatically.

Artificial intelligence

AI features are included; scope and limits depend on the product and plan described by the provider.

Pricing, free plan and trial

Pro from $99/month with 5,000 AI credits, 1 site and 1 user. Pricing scales by credits and sites through larger Pro configurations; Enterprise from $2,500/month. Additional site $59/month and additional user $29/month. Annual billing saves 20%. Free plan: No permanent free plan is confirmed in the current review. Trial: A trial or test period is available; duration and conditions depend on the provider’s current offer.

Strengths

brings traditional SEO and AI visibility together; content, publishing, indexing and outreach in one workflow; MCP; CMS integrations; well-documented affiliate programme; strong fit for BUSCO and publishers

Things to consider

starts at $99/month; credit-based usage; expensive Enterprise tier; GEO is still a fast-moving market; AI outputs and editorial strategy still need human review

Integrations and environment

Integrations: WordPress, Webflow, Framer, Shopifand, Wix, HubSpot, Slack, Zapier, webhooks; withectores Ahrefs, Semrush and other according to availability. MCP for Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Cursor, VS Code and customers compatible. Platform: Web/cloud + MCP. Chrome extension: no; the official page for SEO professionals states that one is not currently offered. Languages: Spanish support is not fully confirmed in the current review.

What makes it different

connects AI-answer visibility to operational actions: identify the opportunity, create content, link it, publish it, index it and run outreach

How to decide if it fits

Sight AI may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
