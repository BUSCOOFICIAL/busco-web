ActiveCampaign: what it does, pricing and alternatives

A marketing automation and CRM platform for automating email, customer journeys, segmentation, sales and customer experience. Its current offering also includes Active Intelligence and AI agents.

What it is useful for

automate marketing and sales; email and SMS; segmentation; lead nurturing and scoring; CRM and pipelines; ecommerce; personalisation; journeys; reporting; AI for campaigns and automations

Who it suits

SMEs, ecommerce businesses, marketers, agencies, SaaS companies and marketing and sales teams that need more advanced automation than a basic newsletter platform

Main features

Core capabilities: automate marketing and sales; email and SMS; segmentation; lead nurturing and scoring; CRM and pipelines; ecommerce; personalisation; journeys; reporting; AI for campaigns and automations.

Artificial intelligence

AI features are included; scope and limits depend on the product and plan described by the provider.

Pricing, free plan and trial

ActiveCampaign uses pricing based on product/plan and number of contacts. Prices change with database size, modules and billing cycle. Check the official pricing page for the intended number of contacts before publishing a fixed BUSCO price. Free plan: No permanent free plan is confirmed in the current review. Trial: A trial or test period is available; duration and conditions depend on the provider’s current offer.

Strengths

powerful automations; advanced segmentation; built-in CRM; broad ecosystem; ecommerce features; growing AI capabilities; mature and widely used product

Things to consider

pricing is more complex than simpler alternatives; cost rises with contacts and features; steeper learning curve than basic tools; some capabilities require higher tiers or add-ons

Integrations and environment

Integrations: more of 1.000 integrations according to the ecosystem official, with Shopifand, WooCommerce, Salesforce, WordPress, Zapier and many tools of sales, ecommerce and productividad. API available. Platform: Web/cloud; mobile apps for CRM, contact and deal management are available on supported iOS/Android devices. Languages: Spanish is supported; the exact interface and content coverage depends on the product.

What makes it different

deep automation and segmentation combined with CRM and a very broad integration ecosystem

How to decide if it fits

ActiveCampaign may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
