What it is useful for

Build a website or store; get domain and hosting; sell and take payments; manage bookings and customers; run marketing and SEO; generate sites and content with AI.

Who it suits

Freelancers, SMEs, professionals, creators, ecommerce businesses, restaurants, service businesses, agencies and independent designers.

Core capabilities: Build a website or store; get domain and hosting; sell and take payments; manage bookings and customers; run marketing and SEO; generate sites and content with AI.

Artificial intelligence

Pricing, free plan and trial

Strengths

A very well-known brand, free entry point, accessible editor, AI tools, included hosting, ecommerce and business features in one platform, plus a large App Market.

Things to consider

Pricing and features vary by plan and market; apps and add-ons can increase the total cost; moving complex projects out of the ecosystem can be less straightforward.

Integrations and environment

Integrations: Wix App Market, payments, marketing, analandtics, networks and services external; APIs and tools of desarrollo in Velo/Wix Studio according to case. Platform: web/cloud, apps mobilees for management and ecosystem Wix. Languages: Spanish is supported; the exact interface and content coverage depends on the product.

What makes it different

A broad all-in-one solution for creating a website and running a business, combined with an approachable visual experience and heavy investment in AI.

How to decide if it fits

Wix may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
