Who it suits

self-employed professionals, SMEs, agencies, operations, HR, sales, customer success and teams that need internal apps or portals connected to their data

Artificial intelligence

Pricing, free plan and trial

Softr offers several tiers, including Free, with paid plans scaling by users, apps, records and features. Exact prices should be taken from the official pricing page at publication because the structure has changed and can use different metrics for internal and external users. Free plan: A free plan is available. Limits and included features depend on the provider’s current plan conditions. Trial: A trial or test period is available; duration and conditions depend on the provider’s current offer.

Strengths

fast no-code app creation; templates; permissions; useful data sources; clear business use cases; AI-assisted building; free plan

Things to consider

limits on users, records and features; some data sources and advanced controls depend on the plan; not a replacement for custom development in complex applications; pricing can become significant as usage grows

Integrations and environment

Integrations: Not detailed in the current profile. Platform: Web/cloud; apps created with Softr are responsive web apps. A native mobile app generated directly by Softr as a general offer: NOT CONFIRMED. Languages: Spanish support is not fully confirmed in the current review.

What makes it different

makes it easy to turn existing data into portals and business apps through a visual no-code layer with AI-assisted generation

How to decide if it fits

Softr may fit if its core functions match the work you need to solve and its limitations do not block your process. Before deciding, test it with a real business case and review integrations, data export and scaling cost. BUSCO! does not rank this profile by commission; the goal is to compare fit.
